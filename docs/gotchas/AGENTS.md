# `docs/gotchas/`

A gotcha is a warning written for agents: a trap that has cost a session a multi-step detour, in three lines: symptom, cause, what to do instead. The bar is that the note would have saved the detour, and that the trap recurs. A one-off diagnosis is not a gotcha.

Entries are deleted once the underlying cause is fixed in code or config.

Skim the filenames and title lines first. Open the one covering your area before you start.
